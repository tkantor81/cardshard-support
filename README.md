# CardShard for Cyberpunk TCG

Browse the full Cyberpunk TCG card database, filter by set, rarity, color, cost and more, and swipe through cards in fullscreen.

CardShard is the unofficial companion app for the Cyberpunk Trading Card Game. Built for collectors, deck builders, and players, it gives you the full card catalog at your fingertips - fast, searchable, and designed for mobile.

**Features:**

- Complete card database - browse every card and every printing
- Advanced filters - filter by set, rarity, color, type, cost, power, RAM, tags, timing triggers, and keyword abilities
- Smart sorting - sort by card number, name, color, type, cost, power, or RAM
- Set-aware printings - switch between card variants and see the matching set, collector number, artist, and legality
- Fullscreen card viewer - swipe left and right to browse cards
- Image caching - card art is cached on your device so you can browse without waiting
- Cyberpunk-inspired UI - dark, neon styling that matches the game’s world
